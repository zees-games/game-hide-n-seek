# game-hide-n-seek

Hide and seek. Most players hide, as themselves in the hiders' blue; one or two seekers count blindfolded and then catch them, by a touch (or, on a server set that way, by keeping them in view), and every hider caught becomes a seeker where they stand. The hiders win if anybody is still hidden when the clock runs out. Built from mg-prop-hunt with disguises, hitboxes and weapons taken out and catching and doors put in.

Read the family [CLAUDE.md](../../CLAUDE.md) first; this file is only what is specific to this game. The maps are in `maps/`, a link to [hide-n-seek-maps](../hide-n-seek-maps), written by its `tools/build_maps.py`; `maps/README.md` there is the format. **The detail is in [docs/](docs/README.md).**

## Running and testing

- `./game.sh` plays offline; `./game.sh online` runs a local server and browser client; `./game.sh test` parses and runs all seven suites. After a map change, `python3 ../hide-n-seek-maps/tools/build_maps.py --check`.
- `headless_maps` checks every toy stands clear and is still where it was put two seconds on (armed: a tyre moved into the fence fails it); `headless_net` kicks a ball during the hide (a seeker is not told) and the seek (it is), and presses the switch; `headless_run` walks into the ball, kicks the football by the use key, bounces on a trampoline and resets at a new round. `tools/shot.sh --view=room` with `--at`/`--look` near a map's toys renders them.
- `headless_maps` swings every door and walks every doorway; `headless_climb` walks every stairway and a tube, and hops into the corner behind every school door. Both need `maps/` linked, and say so when it is not; `headless_climb`'s garden (a tree's branches, a table's legs) needs nothing. [testing](docs/testing.md)
- After adding a model or changing a kit's scale, `godot --headless --path . --script tools/measure_props.gd` (about two minutes: it decomposes every model) rewrites both `props/` files; `-- --check` compares them.
- After touching a camera, the view key or player collision: `tools/camera_check.sh` (cameras round the house, armed; `--render` for frames). [design](docs/design.md#cameras-and-bodies)
- **Render after touching what anyone sees** (`tools/shot.sh`); the shirt colour, HUD overlaps, fog and floor striping were only found in a picture.

## Rules

- **Sound follows the map document**: each box's `mat` is its collision shape's `surface` meta (footsteps by surface, dot-audio's `DotAudioFootsteps`; under 1.4 m/s a step is silent, so creeping is quiet), every `ceiling`/`roof` box is an indoor ambience zone, and the outdoor bed and ground come from `HsAudio.MAP_SOUND` or the document's `ambience`/`surface`. Levels are measured with `addons/dot_audio/tools/loudness.sh` (−17 LUFS band, `loudness_trim_db`).
- **A catch is the server's measurement**: touch (`tag_range`, feet close, no wall between) or sight (cone, range, nothing solid between, a meter). A client sends movement and the use key, nothing a catch could come from. [design](docs/design.md#a-catch-is-a-measurement-and-only-the-server-makes-one)
- People do not collide with people.
- **A bot knows of a person only by its own eyes and ears** (`HsBots._sees`, `_heard`; dot-bot's cone, sight and hearing) and the beacon; never hand it a position. Its pathing mesh is baked without the doors and the survey runs on a fixed tick, so `headless_bots` replays the same; keep both. Because of the first, a bot walks round an open panel's free edge (`HsBots._door_detour`), steps out of a door's swing before using it (the game refuses a use while the opener is in it), and `headless_bots` holds how long one lingers beside a panel to 9 s on 48 seeds (run `-- --first 24` as the second half). A door whose open panel would split a small room slides instead (a camp cabin, a cottage bedroom). [design](docs/design.md#bots)
- **A prop collides as its pieces, or as its box when it has none** (`HsProps.shapes_of`, dot-props' `DotPropPieces`): never hand-place a collision shape; re-measure. The sight test and the walls a body meets are the same shapes. [design](docs/design.md#props-collide-as-their-shape)
- **Holding still is the `HOLD` modifier in the movement state**, never a timer the game checks, so the held client predicts it exactly (the blindfold and a new seeker's freeze both).
- A joiner during the seek seeks; a fall during the seek is a catch (`CAUGHT_FELL`).
- The first caught seeks next (`seeker_pick` `FIRST_CAUGHT`); side wishes are honoured except over that.
- **The blindfold is true on the wire**: `HsInterest` sends a seeker no hider during the hide. The sight meter is `to_owner_only`.
- **A READY before the player is seated is kept** (`_ready_unseated` in the bridge) and answered by `add_player`: over ENet with loss it overtakes the link's `loaded`, and a dropped READY left the client waiting for ever. `headless_net` sends it first (armed). [history](docs/history.md)
- Players and bots are dot-player-char's people (`HsFigure` wraps a `DotHumanoidFigure`; the avatar's blocky atlas letter picks the person, `DotHumanoidLook.from_letter`). The side colour is the whole top, never a tint; a side change rebuilds the figure. [design](docs/design.md)
- **Toys** ([design](docs/design.md#toys)): dot-props' `DotPropToyBox`, built by `HsMap` from the document's `toys`, paired across the wire by index (TOYS names the net ids and the map after MAP; a client registers the mirrors on TOYS, map built or not; `HsToyNet` replicates `state_transform`/`on`/`count`); never predicted. Loose toys are on the `toy` layer, which people do not collide with: `HsGame._advance_toys` pushes them from everybody's capsule and puts weight on see-saws, after the players move. The use key takes a toy looked at (a ray) before a door (`_advance_use`). Trampolines and pads are `HsController._on_pre_simulate` (the `launcher`) and a roundabout's carry `_on_post_simulate` (the `carrier`), so a client predicts both; only the server counts a bounce. Toys are in the sight test (hide behind a barrel), not in a seeker's snapshot during the hide (`HsInterest`), home at every round's start, and baked round in the bots' mesh (`nav_bodies`). `sv_toys*` bound REPLICATED, followed by the client.
- **Doors** ([doors](docs/doors.md)): state is `(open, since, flip)`; only a `DOOR` use is sent (and replayed to joiners); a client poses doors for its input tick before predicting; doors are static bodies on the world layer moved through `PhysicsServer3D`; a use is refused while anybody is in the panel's sweep; the use key is `BUTTON_USER_0` with the press made from the edge.
- **Rooms are pastel on Kenney's prototype grid; furniture keeps Kenney's colours except the ten rebuilt by `art-src/build_props.py` (stock copies in `art-src/base/`, re-run it, bounds are refitted so the catalogue stays)** (`HsMaterials.PASTELS`, map tint paled halfway on top). Light is a dot-lighting document from the map's `environment` (`HsMap.light_document`) drawn with dot-lighting's `painted()` (API 3; contrast 1.04, ambient and fill scaled by the map's `ambient_energy`, the fill re-aimed after the map's sun), glow only above white; the ground outside a step deeper than the walls. A secret panel is drawn with its wall's surface and tint, so it still matches. Boxes and block furniture get dot-lighting's `DotContactShade` (`HsMap._add_contact_shade`, `_shades_floor`). [design](docs/design.md#colour-and-light)
- The client's platform layer (cover, settings, console, Tab, M, pointer, input block) is dot-game's `DotGameClient`, `HsClient.platform`; a suite waits for `platform.is_shown()` and presses Tab through `settings.menu.handle_event`.
- The HUD follows the menu's theme and HUD size (`HsHud.follow_menu`, dot-menu's `follow_hud` by name); `HsHud.piece_theme()` is the accent only (the map blue as highlight and edge), never plate, text or ink, and the HUD's Labels set no colour of their own except the accent lines. `headless_run` checks a theme change reaches the clock and the lines.
- dot-combat's trace is set even though nothing shoots.
- A dead body falls as dot-player-char's ragdoll: the server picks the fall (`HsGame.decide_death`, on `player.death`), it rides DEATH last and optional, and the client copies it from the figure (`HsClient._fall`, from `_on_death`, solo and connected). `sv_ragdoll*` bound in the module, REPLICATED, followed by the client; the death sounds are in `HsAudio`'s catalogue. `headless_run` slays a stand-in and checks the body lies on the floor; `tools/shot.sh --view=ragdoll --seconds=0.5` renders the fall. See dot-player-char's [docs/ragdolls.md](../dot-player-char/docs/ragdolls.md).
- **H is help** (dot-menu's stock key and "H Help" plate; `HsSettings._fill_help` writes the goal and rules), so nothing in `HsBindings` may default to H; the Escape menu has dot-menu's stock Help and Leave. `headless_run` checks both.
- **M is the map list** (dot-menu's map menu): `HsGame.map_rows()` and `change_map_now()` (the next map plus dot-match's `begin_round_now`) serve both solo and the server, where dot-vote's map menu lets the owner's `sv_map_menu_flag` change it now and gives anybody else a vote. It shares the taunt list's picker; a map choice never reaches the taunt handler. Nothing in `HsBindings` may default to M; `headless_run` and `dedicated` check it.
- **Delivery**: no `class_name` in `game/`; every own `res://` path through `HsPaths.rebase()` where defined (see [mg-buses-from-hell's delivery.md](../mg-buses-from-hell/docs/delivery.md)). Maps are their own pack, named by the server owner's map config and fetched by `HsModule._add_delivered_maps`; the game's own pack carries only `hs_practice`. dot-server-deploy's `examples/hideseek_client` is the real-client check.
- **The map vote** (`game/hs_vote.gd`, MANUAL, applied at a round's end): under MANUAL `HsVote` opens a passed rock-the-vote itself (dot-vote does not; `rtv_delay_sec` 0), and `_game_load` refreshes the map list after `_add_delivered_maps`. Without either, `!rtv` answers "Rocked. 1 of 1." and nothing opens, or a delivered server's ballot holds only the practice map and never opens. `examples/dedicated` arms both. The match answers dot-vote's `round_in_progress_fn` (`world.match_node.is_live()`, set by name because an older shell's dot-vote lacks it), so a `time_up: finish_round` limit that runs out in warmup or between rounds changes at once instead of holding for `finish_round_max_sec`; `dedicated` checks both halves.
- **maps.yml** (`cfg/maps.yml` over a shipped `res://maps.yml`; `--config` directory searched; `sv_map_rotation_file`): dot-map's `DotMapRotationFile` decides which installed maps ROTATE. This game keeps maps as documents, so `HsGame.apply_rotation` mirrors a `DotMapDef` per id, applies the file to those and hands `HsCatalogue` the marks (`in_rotation_fn`, `rotation_order`): the draw (`next_map`, in the file's mode) and the vote's ballot and nominations read them, `hs_map <id>` and the M menu still reach an unlisted map, and `hs_maps` and the menu mark it "not in rotation". The `commands:` block renames dot-vote's commands (`commands_spec_fn`) and this game's `hs_map` / `hs_maps` (roles of those names). No file: every map rotates. Pack maps on the ballot are dot-vote's merge and are not filtered by the file (needs a dot-vote hook). `dedicated` holds it.
- **Workshop maps** (`provides.json` at the root: `accepts.kinds` `["map"]`, `map_kinds` `["hideseek"]`, no props because a prop here needs measured pieces): a pack's `map` entry has `map_kind: hideseek` and its `scene` is a map DOCUMENT (JSON, whose `id` must equal the entry's). `HsCatalogue.follow_content` reads `DotContentIndex.shared()` on every `playable()` call, so the solo menu (M), the vote and a change of map all see a subscription and an online session sees none; the game's own ids win. The vote lists pack maps through dot-vote's `follow_content` ("Name [Pack]"), so `HsModule` leaves them out of `maps_fn`. `headless_workshop` holds it; `tools/shot.sh --view=mapmenu --workshop` renders it.
- The catching rules depend on nothing of this game's; extract them to an addon once a second game wants them.
- `project.godot` carries no comments: the editor drops them, and any key left at its default, the first time it saves the file, so the reasons for its settings live here. Gravity stays the engine default and nothing reads it: `HsConfig.gravity` is written onto the world's own physics space, because a project setting does not travel inside a delivered pack. The renderer is `gl_compatibility`, what the delivered client runs, and lighting is tuned under it. The canvas texture filter is Nearest so the prototype grid reads as discrete squares at distance. Texture imports keep `detect_3d/compress_to=0` (`[importer_defaults]` starts new ones that way) so the editor never re-imports one VRAM-compressed on a first open.
- `HsEvent.MAX_BODY` holds the largest MAP (`HsEvents.MAP_BYTES`): a longer body reads as empty with no error, and at 4096 hs_park with its toys reached every delivered client as no map; `headless_net` checks it (armed).
