## Rules

- **Without `maps/` linked** (CI clones this repository alone), `headless_maps` checks the practice house only and `headless_climb` has nothing to climb, and both say so.
- **Check before stepping across a round boundary**: with intermission 0, a check four ticks after the last catch saw the next round.
- **Bound a server round in server ticks**, with the wall clock only as a cap for a server that stopped: a 75 s wall-clock bound failed one run in three with a build running alongside.
- Every behaviour that crosses the wire is armed: the hold as a server-only timer, the door posing taken out, the sweep taken out.

## Renders

`tools/shot.sh --view=overview|room|hider|seeker|blind|caught|ragdoll`, with `--hs-map-ids=<id>`, `--at=x,y,z --look=x,y,z`, `--third`, `--board`, `--doors=open`. Shirt colours, HUD overlaps, wording, fog colour, floor striping and the secret panel's outline were all found by rendering.

## The deployment

dot-server-deploy's `examples/hideseek_client` boots the delivered pack, joins with a real client over a real socket, reads the maps, prop catalogue and taunts from their mounts, and checks the client builds the server's map with every prop.

## pieces.json was re-measured (2026-10-28)

`props/pieces.json` is measured from the rebuilt models of passes 1 to 4 (`measure_props.gd -- --check` passes): a convex piece per decoration and re-cut furniture and trees (the climbing trees' roots and knot are stripped). The tighter hulls leave hiders less cover: over 72 rounds (`-- --first 0|24|48`) the hiders won 24 (6, 8, 10 per 24; was 12 of 24 on the first) and 18% were never found (15, 19, 21%; was 21%), so the bands' floors are 20% and 10% (were 30% and 15%), a measured shift and not a loosening: a hider that stands or a seeker that finds nobody still fails the armed rounds and the ceilings. Weakest maps: `hs_manor`, `hs_apartments`, `hs_village`, `hs_park` (hiders won 1 of 11, 2 of 11, 1 of 10, 1 of 10); `hs_camp`, `hs_school`, `hs_restaurant` favour them. `headless_climb` 47/0, `headless_bots` 18/0 on seed 0.

## Cut-off hider spawns (2026-11-03)

`headless_maps` checks that every hider spawn is joined to the first seeker spawn on the bots' walkable mesh. `hs_ship`, `hs_apartment`, `hs_frontyard` and `hs_hotel` fail it and are held in `CUT_OFF_BASELINE` (the check fails if one becomes joined, so the entry is removed). `hs_frontyard` bots catch 2 of 4 hiders in every round (the other 2 are never found) and `hs_apartment` hiders last 7-13 s (below the 90 s band), so neither can join `headless_bots` DEFAULT_MAPS until their spawns are joined.

## hs_frontyard and hs_apartment, measured 2026-11-03

`headless_bots` rerun on a quiet box passes 18/0 (the 1500 s cap on a loaded box was load, not a regression). `HS_BOTS_MAPS=hs_frontyard,hs_apartment` with the maps' own `players_per_seeker 2.0` makes 3 seekers: apartment hiders last 16-35 s. With `players_per_seeker` 8.0 (apartment) and 4.0 (frontyard) and apartment `seeker_pace` 0.15 they last 66-138 s and frontyard 110-196 s, but the 24 rounds still fail five checks (hiders win 17 of 24, a catch at 1.3-1.5 s on frontyard, walk-off 94 of 127, a bot stuck 66.7 s at apartment (0.4, 0.3, 17.8), coverage armed round 19%), so neither joins `DEFAULT_MAPS`. Those values belong in the maps repository and are not applied.
