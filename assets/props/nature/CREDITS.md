# Nature models

Twelve of the models here are this game's own, built from Kenney's Nature Kit by `art-src/build_props.py` (Blender in background mode: `blender -b -P art-src/build_props.py -- <name>`): `tree_default`, `tree_oak`, `tree_tall`, `tree_cone`, `tree_pineTallA`, `tree_pineRoundA`, `tree_detailed`, `tree_fat`, `tree_small`, `plant_bushLarge`, `plant_bushDetailed`, `crop_melon`. The rest of the folder is the kit as Kenney made it.

- **Base**: [Kenney's Nature Kit](https://kenney.nl/assets/nature-kit) by Kenney (CC0 1.0; licence in `LICENSE-kenney-nature-kit.txt`). The unmodified originals are in `art-src/base/nature/`.
- **Changes**: our own bark, a three-tone foliage by height with noise (greens for leaf trees, blue-greens for pines), a moss patch on each trunk, blossom, apples, oranges or pine cones scattered on the canopy, berries on the bushes, a soil disc under them, halved melons on the patch.
- **Kept**: node names and tree, origin and bounding box (refitted to the stock box), so `props/catalogue.json` is unchanged. `props/pieces.json` is not re-measured (see `docs/testing.md`, "pieces.json is stale"): a re-measure adds pieces for the decoration and moves `headless_bots`' hiders' win rate below its band.
- **Pass 4 (2026-10-28)**: `rock_largeA`, `rock_tallA`, `rock_smallA`, `log`, `log_large`, `log_stack`, `crop_pumpkin` are ours too, from the same base ([Kenney's Nature Kit](https://kenney.nl/assets/nature-kit), Kenney, CC0 1.0; originals in `art-src/base/nature/`): three-tone stone with moss and lichen, pebbles; two-tone bark, growth rings and moss, a toadstool; a ribbed pumpkin with a stem and a small one beside it.
